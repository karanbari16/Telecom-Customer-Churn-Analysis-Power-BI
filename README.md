📌 Problem Statement: Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

In the highly competitive telecom industry, customer retention is a major challenge as companies frequently experience loss of customers (churn) due to various factors such as pricing, service quality, contract type, and customer experience.

The organization is facing difficulty in identifying:

Why customers are leaving
Which customers are more likely to churn
What factors contribute most to customer attrition

Currently, the available data is not being effectively utilized to extract meaningful insights, making it hard for decision-makers to take proactive actions to reduce churn.

🎯 Objective:

To analyze telecom customer data using Power BI and develop an interactive dashboard that:

Identifies key drivers of customer churn
Segments customers based on risk levels
Tracks churn trends across different demographics and services
Provides actionable insights to improve customer retention strategies


📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis (Power BI)

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior in the telecom industry using an interactive Power BI dashboard. The goal is to identify key factors influencing customer attrition and provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.

🔍 Key Features:
📉 Churn Rate Analysis – Overall percentage of customers who have left the service
👥 Customer Segmentation – Analysis based on demographics like gender, tenure, and customer type
📊 Churn by Services – Impact of services such as internet type, phone service, and add-ons on churn
💳 Payment & Contract Insights – Churn comparison across contract types (monthly/yearly) and payment methods
⏳ Tenure Analysis – Understanding how customer retention varies with duration
⚠️ High-Risk Customer Identification – Detect patterns of customers likely to churn
🔄 Interactive Dashboard – Dynamic filters for deeper analysis

🛠️ Tools & Technologies:
Power BI
Data Modeling
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Data Cleaning & Transformation
